🥚 Easter Quest 🐰
The eggs are hidden, the clock is ticking, and the Easter Spirit needs YOU!
Somebody hid every Easter egg in town and forgot where they put them! 😱 follow the cheerful little Easter Spirit, and start a colourful low-poly egg hunt that runs straight from your browser. No download needed!

🌙 Level 1: The Dark Room. The lights are out and the eggs are hiding somewhere in the shadows. Can you spot them before time runs out?

🎡 Level 2: The Funfair. Ferris wheels, carousels, snack stalls and signs. The eggs are hiding all over the rides, so look under, behind and on top of everything!

🌷 Level 3: The Secret Garden. The hardest level yet. The eggs are tucked behind rocks and bushes, and only the sharpest hunters will find them all.

✨ The Final Mystery. Make it this far and one last surprise is waiting: the legendary Golden Egg. 🏆

💡 Stuck? Earn a Hint!
Press H and solve a quick brain-teaser, like a word puzzle or a math question. Answer correctly and the closest egg will bounce and glow to show you where it is. Answer wrong and you lose that hint, so think fast! ⏱️ Collect golden stars to earn extra hints along the way.

🎮 Controls
WASD	Move
Mouse	Look around
Space	Jump
Shift	Sprint
E	Pick up egg
H	Hint
P	Pause
